# AEGIS product steering

Primary actor: a regional poultry distributor in Missouri. AEGIS Command supports dispatcher evidence review and scenario comparison. AEGIS Drive presents approved assignments for use while parked. All simulated contracts, shipments, lots, and telemetry must remain labeled.

Route testing also supports a single farm dispatch origin and fleets operating from multiple locations through shared operational presets. These are planning experiments; enterprise/farm account modes and live sensor connectors remain P2. Use `docs/priority_matrix.md` to protect the P0 submission before adding differentiators.

Safety decisions require human approval. Predictive positivity is not consumer illness probability. CDC surveillance and historical outbreaks remain separate from FSIS labels unless a defensible linkage is established.

# AEGIS core requirements

Status: preparatory specification written by Codex; not reviewed or executed in AWS Kiro.

1. When a data directory is supplied, the system shall recursively inventory supported files without modifying them and record exact read failures while continuing other sources.
2. When a public source is queried, the system shall preserve retrieval time, response schema, status, and provenance and shall expose missing/stale coverage.
3. When simulated logistics are displayed, the system shall identify fictional entities and show a deterministic Missouri network.
4. When weather is displayed, the system shall distinguish observations, forecasts, reanalysis, and simulated cargo temperature.
5. When routing fails, the system shall provide a visibly estimated fallback and shall not claim truck-safe navigation.
6. When a plan is generated, the system shall enforce inventory, capacity, destination demand, eligibility, hold/recall, deadline, and shelf-life constraints.
7. When a consequential action is proposed, the system shall require explicit dispatcher approval, preserve an audit trail, and reject stale proposals.
8. When a plan is approved, the system shall publish simulated driver assignments and allow acknowledgment while parked.
9. When model results are displayed, the system shall show measured chronological validation results and availability assumptions, without interpreting positivity as illness probability.
10. When CDC evidence is used, it shall remain separate from FSIS outcomes without a scientifically supported linkage.
11. When a real road incident is relevant to a route, the system shall generate a review alert using validated geometry proximity/intersection, without claiming commercial-truck validation.
12. When an operator tests farm or regional fleet routes, the system shall support one or multiple dispatch locations using explicitly simulated planning presets without claiming account isolation or real commercial relationships.
13. When cargo-temperature projections are displayed, the system shall identify their assumptions and simulation status and shall not present an unvalidated thermal projection as a probability of illness or a regulatory decision.
14. When release work is selected, the team shall complete the P0 provenance, estimator/baseline, intervention, actual Kiro workflow and report/code/demo gates before P2 assistants, account modes or live sensors.

Acceptance evidence is tracked in `docs/progress.md`; unverified requirements remain open.

# Save the Load: quick operator walkthrough

Open **Save the Load** from the sidebar. Generic entry chooses the documented
demonstration candidate; an explicit Command shipment link carries that trip.
The workspace does not create an experiment or publish an assignment merely
because you opened it.

1. Select **Start rescue experiment**. The candidate, lot, truck and source are
   shown together. The demonstration uses a near-expiry quality budget so its
   response can be presented in a short, accelerated replay.
2. Select **Replay heat excursion** for one controlled event. **Play sensor
   replay** instead starts the normal/heat sequence and pauses when a checked
   rescue proposal is ready. Those two paths have different elapsed scenario
   times, so read their displayed values rather than assuming identical clocks.
3. Compare **Point quality-window estimate**, **Original destination requires**
   and **Conservative sensitivity window**. Point and sensitivity estimates use
   separate values. The range is chosen-parameter sensitivity, not a confidence
   interval or a guarantee of safety.
4. Inspect the short priority destination list. **Show all N candidates and
   exclusions** expands the complete captured list. A buyer is offered for human
   review only when both modeled eligibility and dispatch eligibility pass.
   Excluded choices remain inspectable; a hub without confirmed receiving space
   stays inspection-only.
5. **Check road route** deliberately requests a road comparison. A fallback
   remains labeled as an estimated connection. This action can refine travel
   estimates; it does not approve a recipient, truck or assignment.
6. For a different eligible destination, select **Prepare selected rescue**.
   **Review [destination]** then opens the complete proposed assignment plan,
   including lot, truck, destination, quantity, cost and checked constraints.
7. Enter the reviewing dispatcher and approval reason. Confirm the separate
   quality/disposition, receiving and route/vehicle reviews, then select
   **Approve simulated rescue plan**. Approval replaces the whole checked
   simulated assignment list. **Open approved driver assignments** carries the
   captured truck and shipment into Drive.

The warehouse panel compares actual FIFO and FEFO solver picks. Use **Show FEFO
pick order** and **Show FIFO pick order** to inspect lot order and quantities.
Stock exposed to modeled expiry is a planning measure, not observed food waste.

**Follow incoming readings every 5s** polls stored captures; it does not invent
new observations. Product/model temperature and ambient temperature remain
separate. Humidity, vibration and ethylene are displayed as context channels,
without unsupported poultry decay coefficients. No physical sensor connection,
calibration or real lot traceability is established by the replay.

New readings, a changed selection or a different network revision disable an
older approval form. **Refresh plan for new review** preserves accumulated
exposure history while updating its decision context. A previous receipt stays
historical, with **Inspect driver assignment context** rather than a claim of a
current approval. A failed refresh does not erase a confirmed decision.

After a reviewed rescue, **Continue lot's exposure history** opens the same linked
experiment. A new experiment cannot reset its consumed quality age; older
counterfactual sessions cannot overwrite it. Use a fresh isolated rehearsal to
repeat the original demonstration.

**Export captured freshness experiment** saves the complete received snapshot,
source labels and model assumptions. Unsent reviewer text is not inserted into
it. These are conditional, uncalibrated quality estimates; they do not measure
molecular freshness, establish pathogen safety or extend supplied use-by caps.
